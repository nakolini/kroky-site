Kroky: Walk Breaks

You've sat 2 hours. It's dry until 17:50. Here's a 25-min park loop.

Walk breaks for desk workers, on iPhone.

App Store: coming soon

How it works

  1. Kroky reads your steps from Apple Health and notices when you've been sitting.
  2. It checks the hourly forecast and waits for a dry window.
  3. It plans a walking loop through nearby parks, sized to close the gap to today's step goal.
  4. One tap hands the loop to Google Maps or Apple Maps for turn-by-turn directions.

Private by design

On your iPhone

Your steps, settings and walk history stay on the device. There is no Kroky server.

No account

Open the app and set a goal. There is nothing to sign up for.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no tracking SDKs. Location is used only while you use the app.
