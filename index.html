Kroky: Walk Breaks

You've sat 2 hours. It's dry until 17:50. Here's a 25-min walk.

Walk breaks for desk workers, on iPhone.

App Store: coming soon

How it works

  1. Kroky reads your steps from Apple Health and notices when you've been sitting.
  2. It checks the hourly forecast and waits for a dry window.
  3. It plans a walk from your door and back, sized to close the gap to today's step goal, and comes back a different way when the streets allow.
  4. Tap Go and Kroky shows the way on a live map, even with the screen locked. Google Maps or Apple Maps are one tap away if you prefer them.

Private by design

On your iPhone

Your steps, settings and walk history stay on the device. There is no Kroky server.

No account

Open the app and set a goal. There is nothing to sign up for.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no tracking SDKs. Location is used only while Kroky is open, for the weather and to plan or follow a walk.
