How long do I need to walk to reach my step goal?

At an easy walking pace of about 100 steps a minute, 1,000 steps take about 10 minutes and cover about 750 m (0.5 mi). So 3,000 steps left is a walk of about 30 minutes and 2.2 km (1.4 mi).

With a stride of 0.75 m and a pace of 80 m a minute. Taller people and faster walkers cover more ground per step and per minute.

Your own numbers

Stride and pace differ from person to person. Kroky starts from your walking step length in Apple Health and, after two walks with Kroky, uses the stride and pace from your own walks, so the steps it plans match the steps Health counts.

Let Kroky do the maths

Home shows the steps you have left today. Tap Close gap and Kroky plans a loop from where you are that's just long enough, or pick 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes.
