Kroky › Tips

A sitting reminder on iPhone, without an Apple Watch

Stand reminders are part of Apple Watch; the iPhone has no built-in reminder to get up. Kroky adds one: it reads the steps your iPhone already counts in Apple Health and, after a long stretch without them, nudges you with a walk sized to the steps you still need, when it's dry outside.

How it differs from Apple Watch stand reminders

A stand reminder asks you to stand and move for a minute each hour. Kroky is about walking: it waits for a long stretch without steps (90 minutes by default), checks the weather, and comes with a loop from where you are, sized to close the gap to today's step goal. It nudges at most twice a day, only in the hours you set, and can stay quiet during your meetings.

What it needs

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Your iPhone counts steps on its own while you carry it.
  • Permission to read steps from Apple Health. If you also wear an Apple Watch, its steps count too.
  • Notifications, and the hours and days Kroky may nudge you.

Good to know

Sitting nudges are part of Kroky Pro, which starts with a 7-day free trial; the free plan gives you a new loop every day when you open the app. Steps only count while your iPhone is with you, so if it stays on the desk while you walk around, Kroky can think you've been sitting. Kroky doesn't log stand hours or replace your Activity rings.
