Kroky

Kroky press kit

Kroky is an iPhone app that notices when you've been sitting, waits for dry weather and plans a walking loop from where you are, just long enough to close the gap to today's step goal.

Fact sheet

  • Name: Kroky: Walk Breaks ("kroky" means "steps" in Ukrainian, Czech and Slovak)
  • Platform: iPhone, iOS 17 or later. On the App Store since November 3, 2026.
  • Price: free. Kroky Pro adds sitting nudges, dry-window reminders, Skip my meetings and unlimited loops: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $49.99 once (US prices).
  • Privacy: no account, no server, no analytics, no tracking. Steps, settings and walk history stay on the iPhone.
  • Languages: 49 languages.
  • Made by: nakolini, a two-person studio of Dmytro Hryb and Vitalii Kikot.
