Support

Help, questions and bug reports.

Common questions

Why doesn't Kroky show my steps?

Kroky reads steps from Apple Health. If Home says "Kroky can't see your steps", it assumes you've been sitting.

To turn access on, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps under Privacy. Choose Kroky and turn on Steps and Walking Step Length. Also check that Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness › Fitness Tracking is on, so your iPhone counts steps at all.

Why am I not getting nudges?

Check these, in order:

Kroky Pro. Sitting nudges and weather reminders are part of Kroky Pro.
Nudges are on. In Kroky, open Settings (the gear on Home) and check that "Nudges and weather reminders" is on, and how long you have to sit before one comes.
Nudge hours and days. Kroky only nudges between the hours and on the days set under "Nudge me between". With Skip my meetings on, it also stays quiet when your calendar shows you as busy.
Notifications. In the iOS Settings app › Notifications › Kroky, allow notifications.

Kroky sends at most 2 nudges a day. iOS decides exactly when the app can check in the background, so a nudge can come a little later than expected, and opening Kroky now and then helps.

Why can't Kroky plan a loop here?

Kroky needs your location and a connection to plan a loop. If it says "Couldn't build a loop of this length here", try a shorter or longer walk, or Shuffle. In places with few streets or paths, it may offer a walk there and back instead.

How do I restore Kroky Pro on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the Apple Account that bought Kroky Pro. Then, in Kroky, open Settings (the gear on Home) and tap Restore Purchases in the Kroky Pro section. The Restore Purchases link on the Kroky Pro screen does the same. This works for both the yearly plan and the lifetime unlock.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?

In Kroky, open Settings › Manage Subscription. Or, in the iOS Settings app, tap your name › Subscriptions › Kroky. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date and you won't be charged again. Kroky Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, or until the free trial ends. Apple handles all billing and refunds, so for a refund go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

The lifetime unlock is a single payment, not a subscription, so there's nothing to cancel.

How do I remove an avoided place?

In Kroky, open Settings › Avoided places. Swipe left on a place and tap Remove, or tap Clear All to remove every one. Future loops may use those streets again.

What happens to my data?

It stays on your iPhone. Kroky has no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy for what goes to Apple and OpenStreetMap to plan loops and check the weather.
