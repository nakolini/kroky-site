How to get 10,000 steps a day with a desk job

Split the gap into walks. At an easy pace, 1,000 steps take about 10 minutes, so if a desk day leaves you at 4,000 steps, two walks of about 30 minutes close the gap to 10,000. Kroky reads the steps you still need and plans a loop of that length from where you are.

Do the math once

Look up in Apple Health how many steps you usually have by the end of a workday, subtract them from 10,000 and divide by 100: that's roughly how many minutes of walking you need. 6,000 steps left is about an hour: two 30-minute walks, or three of 20.

Fit the walks into the day

  • Walk at lunch, when you'd stop anyway.
  • Take a second walk in the afternoon, after a long stretch at the desk.
  • Go when it's dry: a walk planned for the rain is the one you skip.
  • Pick a goal you can keep. One you reach most days does more than one you rarely reach; Kroky offers 6,000, 8,000 and 10,000 steps.

Let Kroky plan them

Home shows the steps you have left today. Tap Close gap for a loop that's just long enough, or pick 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes to split the gap into shorter walks. With Kroky Pro, Kroky nudges you after a long sit, when it's dry.
