Walk safely

Kroky plans routes automatically from Apple Maps and OpenStreetMap data. That data can be out of date or wrong: a path can be closed, private, unlit, muddy, steep or cross a busy road without a crossing. You decide where and when it's safe to walk. Follow traffic rules and local signs, keep your eyes on your surroundings rather than the screen, and don't walk where you don't feel safe.

Weather forecasts come from Apple Weather and can be wrong. Dress for the weather you see, not only the one forecast.

Not medical advice

Kroky is a general wellness app. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Step counts, distances and times are estimates. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or haven't been active for a while, talk to a doctor before you start walking more.
