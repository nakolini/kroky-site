Walk breaks for desk workers · iPhone

You've sat two hours. It's dry until 5:50 PM. Here's a 25-minute walk.

Kroky notices when you've been sitting, waits for dry weather and plans a loop from where you are, sized to close the gap to today's step goal. Tap Go and it guides you, every turn.

Free to start · No account · iPhone, iOS 17 or later

  • Steps from Apple Health
  • Dry-weather windows
  • No tracking

See Kroky

How it works

  1. 1

    It notices you've been sitting

    Kroky reads your steps from Apple Health. After a long stretch without steps (90 minutes unless you change it), it's time for a walk.

  2. 2

    It waits for dry weather

    It checks the hourly forecast and finds today's dry window, so the walk doesn't start in the rain.

  3. 3

    It plans a loop sized to your gap

    From where you are and back, long enough to close the gap to today's step goal, or 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. It goes through parks and quiet streets where it can, and comes back a different way when the streets allow.

  4. 4

    Tap Go and walk

    Kroky guides you on a live map, with spoken directions if you like, even with the screen locked. Apple Maps or Google Maps are one tap away if you prefer them.

What Kroky does

  • Sitting nudges

    A notification after a long sit, only in the hours and on the days you choose, at most twice a day, and only when it's dry out.

  • Today's dry window

    Home shows how long it stays dry, from Apple Weather's hourly forecast. Kroky can remind you when a dry window opens.

  • A loop that closes the gap

    Steps turn into distance with your own stride, learned from your walks. The loop is just long enough for the steps you have left today.

  • Every turn, right in Kroky

    Follow the loop on a live map with the walked part greyed out, distance and time left, and optional spoken directions.

  • Loops that get better

    Shuffle for a different loop, reverse it, rate it, keep the ones you like and mark a street to avoid. Kroky won't offer the same new loop twice in a week.

  • Your week at a glance

    Walks this week, steps added, a streak, each walk's result and your walk history with every route.

  • Skip my meetings

    With your permission, Kroky checks your calendar on your iPhone and stays quiet while you're busy.

  • Miles or kilometres

    Distances follow your iPhone's region settings. Kroky speaks 49 languages.

Private by design

  • On your iPhone

    Your steps, settings and walk history stay on the device. There is no Kroky server.

  • No account

    Open the app and set a goal. There is nothing to sign up for.

  • No tracking

    No analytics, no ads, no tracking SDKs. Location is used while Kroky is open, and during a walk you start, until you end it.

Questions people ask

What is Kroky?

Kroky: Walk Breaks is an iPhone app for people who sit a lot. It notices when you've been sitting, checks when it's dry outside and plans a walking loop from where you are, sized to close the gap to today's step goal. Then it guides you around the loop on a live map.

Is there an app that reminds me to take a walk when I've been sitting too long?

Yes. Kroky reads your steps from Apple Health and, after a long stretch without steps, sends a nudge with a walk ready to go. You choose the hours, the days and how long a sit counts as too long; the default is 90 minutes, between 9:00 and 18:00 on weekdays. It sends at most two nudges a day and only when it's dry. Sitting nudges are part of Kroky Pro.

Which app plans a walking route to reach my daily step goal?

Kroky does. It takes the steps you still need today, turns them into a distance with your own stride, and plans a loop that starts and ends where you are and is just that long. You can also pick a 15, 30, 45 or 60-minute walk instead.

Can Kroky find a walking loop near me that avoids main roads?

It tries to. Kroky looks for parks and footpaths around you with Apple Maps and OpenStreetMap, plans the loop through them and keeps off busy streets where the map allows. If you don't like a stretch, mark it and future loops will avoid it.

Does Kroky check the weather before suggesting a walk?

Yes. It uses Apple Weather's hourly forecast to find today's dry window and shows how long it stays dry. Nudges only come when it's dry, and Kroky Pro can remind you when a dry window opens.

Is Kroky free?

Kroky is free to download and use, with one new loop and one shuffle a day, today's dry window, your steps, live guidance, your weekly view and walk history. Kroky Pro adds sitting nudges, weather reminders, Skip my meetings, unlimited loops and shuffles, and your favorite loops mixed back in. It's a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the app, allow Apple Health access and set a daily goal of 6,000, 8,000 or 10,000 steps.

Does Kroky track my location?

Only when you ask it to. Kroky uses your location while the app is open, to plan a loop and get the weather, and continuously only during a walk you start, from Go until you end it. It never asks for "Always" location and never tracks you outside a walk. Your routes stay on your iPhone.

Does it work with Apple Watch?

Kroky has no Apple Watch app, but it reads your steps from Apple Health, which counts the steps from your iPhone and your Apple Watch together. So a walk with your watch on counts toward your goal.

Does Kroky guide me during the walk?

Yes. Tap Go and Kroky shows where you are on the loop, the part you've walked, and the distance and time left. Spoken directions are optional, and guidance keeps working with the screen locked. You can also open the loop in Apple Maps or Google Maps.

Is Kroky on Android or iPad?

Not yet. Kroky is for iPhone, with iOS 17 or later.

Where does Kroky work?

Anywhere Apple Maps has walking directions. Loops are best where there are parks and footpaths nearby; in places with few, Kroky plans the quietest loop it can, or a walk there and back.

What languages is Kroky in?

49: English, Arabic, Bengali, Catalan, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, Finnish, French (France and Canada), German, Greek, Gujarati, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Kannada, Korean, Malay, Malayalam, Marathi, Norwegian, Odia, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Punjabi, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), Swedish, Tamil, Telugu, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Urdu and Vietnamese, with British and Australian English.

What does the name Kroky mean?

"Steps." It's the Ukrainian word кроки written in Latin letters, and the Czech and Slovak word as it is.
