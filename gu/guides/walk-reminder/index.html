Kroky › Guides

An app that reminds you to walk when you've sat too long

Kroky is an iPhone app that reads your steps from Apple Health, notices a long stretch without them and, when it's dry outside, nudges you with a walk that's ready to go: a loop from where you are, sized to close the gap to today's step goal.

Why a step-based reminder works better than a timer

A timer nudges you every hour whether or not you've moved. Kroky counts real steps, so a walk to a meeting or the kitchen resets the clock, and a nudge only comes when you've really been still. It also waits for a dry window and stays inside the hours you set, so a reminder is a walk you can take now, not one more alert to dismiss.

Setting it up

  1. Allow Kroky to read steps from Apple Health. It only reads; it never writes.
  2. Pick a daily goal: 6,000, 8,000 or 10,000 steps.
  3. Set the hours and days Kroky may nudge you, for example 9:00 to 18:00 on weekdays.
  4. In Settings, choose how long a sit counts as too long (90 minutes to start), and turn on Skip my meetings if you want quiet during calendar events.

Good to know

Sitting nudges are part of Kroky Pro; the free plan gives you a new loop every day when you open the app. Kroky sends at most two nudges a day. iOS decides exactly when an app can check in the background, so a nudge can come a little later than expected.
